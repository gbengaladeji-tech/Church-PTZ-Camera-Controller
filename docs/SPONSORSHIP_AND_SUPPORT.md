# Sponsorship and Project Support

This page records **confirmed** project support only.

It should not be used to list companies merely because an outreach email was sent or because a sponsorship discussion is still in progress.

## Confirmed support

### OSH Cut

**Support type:** fabrication sponsorship / discounted manufacturing support

OSH Cut has confirmed support for the Church PTZ Camera Controller through a project fabrication discount arrangement for eligible orders through the end of 2026.

Their support is expected to be most useful for durable fabricated parts such as a metal faceplate, brackets, or other parts where sheet-metal fabrication materially improves the final design.

The project will acknowledge OSH Cut in documentation and presentations where appropriate.

**Privacy rule:** private sponsor discount codes, account-specific details, and other non-public terms must never be committed to this repository.

## How future support should be recorded

For each confirmed sponsor or contributor, document:

- organization
- type of support
- intended project use
- date confirmed
- any public acknowledgement requirement
- what was actually used in the final build

Do not present product inquiries, discounts still under review, technical conversations, or unanswered outreach as confirmed sponsorship.

## Funding boundary

Project-specific contributions should be used for the PTZ controller according to the agreed purpose. They should not be represented as scholarship or personal university funding.
