# Onshape build guide

Build an editable **preliminary** console using the coordinate workbook. No Onshape document or enclosure STEP was created in this run. Keep uncertain cuts suppressed and marked HOLD. Dimensions are millimetres.

1. **Set up the document.** Create `Church PTZ - preliminary DFM HOLD`. Add Part Studios MASTER, BODY, CONTROL PANEL, REAR PANEL, BOTTOM and BRACKETS, plus an Assembly. All use the same origin. Create an initial version labelled `PRELIMINARY - NOT FOR FABRICATION`.

2. **Enter variables in MASTER.** Use W=400 mm, S=230 mm, a=10 deg, Hf=55 mm, t=1.6256 mm, Ri=1.5748 mm, flange=15 mm, K=0.38, coat=0.0762 mm, profileTol=0.127 mm and jointGap=0.5 mm. Derived variables: `D=#S*cos(#a)` and `Hr=#Hf+#S*sin(#a)`. Expected D=226.506 and Hr=94.939. K, finish thickness, profile tolerance and seams are planning values awaiting Protocase confirmation.

3. **Establish the outer section.** Global origin is the front-left base corner: X right, Y rear, Z up. On the Right/YZ plane sketch the outer trapezoid (Y,Z): (0,0), (D,0), (D,Hr), (0,Hf). Constrain the sloping line from (0,Hf) to (D,Hr). Fully dimension using variables. These are external skin datums.

4. **Create the sloped plane.** Make a front top construction line from (0,0,Hf) to (W,0,Hf) in the Front/XZ plane. In the Right/YZ sketch retain the rear-left top point (0,D,Hr). Use Plane / Three point through those three top corners. The normal should point outward/up. Local panel origin is its front-left corner, local X right and local Y uphill. A panel point (X,s,n) maps to world (X, s*cos(a)-n*sin(a), Hf+s*sin(a)+n*cos(a)).

5. **Make the removable top.** Derive the master geometry in CONTROL PANEL. Sketch W x S on the sloped plane; extrude inward by t as a new part. Add a proposed M3 hole pattern: X=12,137,263,388 at local Y=8 and 222. Trial diameter 3.4 is HOLD pending finished-panel and stock-hardware review. Avoid countersinking this thin plate.

6. **Locate the controls.** Add a sketch `HOLD CONTROL CENTRES` using every row of the workbook's Layout sheet: 34 keys, six encoders, one display and one joystick. CAM1-5: X=190,212,234,256,278 at Y=48; CAM6-10 at Y=26. Presets form four columns X=30,64,98,132, with Y=70,48,26 in each. P1-P3 are the first column, then P4-P6, etc. These are visual groups; all address the selected camera. Other centres are individually listed in the workbook. Do not scale an old drawing.

7. **Draw operating envelopes before holes.** Use 17.75 mm squares for all 27 APEM bezels; 12.15 mm maximum cap squares for seven B32 keys; diameter 28 mm operating allocations around six actual diameter-20 knobs; 102 x 60 mm display allowance at (230,182); diameter-100 trial joystick hand circle at (340,138), plus the actual 53.594 flange. AUTO FOCUS at (38,96) is below FOCUS at (38,128). Positions and hand clearance still need owner testing.

8. **Keep aperture trials separate.** Add a suppressed `HOLD APERTURES` feature group. APEM's 14.7 square is a minimum uncoated family opening. B32 12.8 square and encoder diameter 7.5 are trial coupon values, not production cuts. An M7 encoder uses a clearance opening, not a tapped panel. Determine the finished opening, coating loss on two walls, negative profile tolerance, registration and retention from actual parts. For illustration only, 14.7+2(0.0762)+0.127=14.9794 before registration. A 15.0 coupon does not release the selected APEM assembly.

9. **Mount the display by its PCB datum.** PCB centre (230,182), outline 95 x 47.6. Four PCB holes: (185.7,161.4), (274.3,161.4), (185.7,202.6), (274.3,202.6), diameter 3.2. These locate supports; they do not automatically become visible panel holes. Trial window 70.5 x 42.6 is centred at (232.73,182). The X offset is calculated from one official active-area interpretation; conflicting 69.60/69.90 widths and the actual owned revision must be resolved. Add a removable bezel/gasket only after measuring the real unit and mated cable.

10. **Mount the joystick without guessing its opening.** The four part holes are diameter 2.54; Y pitch 32.512 is dimensioned. Proposed centres (323.744,121.744), (356.256,121.744), (323.744,154.256), (356.256,154.256) assume equal X pitch, which remains HOLD. Candidate M2 hardware only; do not enlarge the joystick. The 39.116 ring is not a manufacturer panel-cutout recommendation. Model known depth 28.575 below flange underside and 49.5046 known body width, then add measured second-axis, tab, wire and swept volumes. Start with 40 mm normal interior allowance, then prove actual clearance and panel stiffness.

11. **Create the chassis mockup.** Use outer datums to model a wrapped front/sidewall chassis with inward top/bottom support flanges, rear frame, removable top and bottom. Offset skins inward by t, use 15 mm trial flanges, trim intersecting corners and leave 0.5 mm trial cover gaps. Corner joints, reliefs and economical folded construction need Protocase DFM. Onshape Sheet Metal tools may be used, but extruded skin mockups and guessed K-factor flat patterns are not manufacturing files.

12. **Create the rear frame and cover.** Rear plane is world Y=D. Proposed cover extent X=10 to390, Z=10 to85 (380 x75); frame opening X=18 to382, Z=18 to77 (364 x59). Six trial M3 centres: X=16,200,384 at Z=16 and79. Check actual seam, bend and driver access before accepting these dimensions. All rear coordinates use global X as seen from the front. In a rear-view drawing, mirror: Xrear=400-Xglobal.

13. **Reserve rear I/O volumes.** Proposed global (X,Z): four serial ports (40,42), (66,42), (92,42), (118,42); LAN (258,42); DC (310,42); USB (354,42). Serial jack reference opening is 16.129 x13.208, radius0.254, uncoated. Finished cuts and PCB datum remain HOLD. LAN uses the W55's integrated jack, not that serial-jack aperture. Select exact DC and USB bulkhead/access parts before cutting. Label RS-422 ports clearly `NOT LAN / NO PoE` and keep LAN distinct.

14. **Make the removable bottom.** Preliminary full cover W xD. Eight M3 trial centres: X=12,137,263,388 at Y=8 and218. Lower chassis flanges support it. Final panel edge gaps depend on seams/finish. Four trial foot positions: (25,25), (375,25), (25,D-25), (375,D-25). Foot type, height, load and attachment remain HOLD.

15. **Define internal supports.** Start with a removable base tray for main/RS-422/power electronics and a 120 x90 x30 mm main-board reservation above the base. Reserve 35 mm normal depth for switch boards and 25 mm for encoder boards, with an empty joystick underside volume. These are allowances, not board designs. APEM, B32 and encoder mounting planes may differ; do not force them onto one PCB height. Specify the tray-to-case interface before shell fabrication. PCB outlines, support grid and actual standoff lengths are still open.

16. **Import the authentic W55 component.** Use the manufacturer's STEP link in Components. Local origin: PCB Ethernet-end corner; X toward USB, Y across width, top Z=0. CAD-derived end holes (78,3.5) and (78,17.5), diameter2.0; header rows Y=1.61 and19.39, 20 holes at X=27.37+2.54i, i=0..19. The full model is 85.35 x21 x16.6 before sockets/plugs. Define socket/bracket support and accessible BOOTSEL, RESET, SWD and USB. Actual received revision must match. This imported component is not a completed enclosure.

17. **Add wiring and service access.** Start with 35 mm internal rear connector allowance, then use actual mated plugs and withdrawal paths. JST XH reference assembled height is9.8 mm; an additional10 mm bend/grip space is only a trial. Add at least50 mm trial harness surplus beyond the actual route and check that it cannot enter the joystick or get pinched. Supports must react connector withdrawal force. Rear I/O must be removable after disconnecting labelled internal harnesses.

18. **Assemble and check.** Fix the chassis in Assembly; mate covers, brackets and components from their datums. Run native interference detection and section views. Record static clearance, full joystick movement, hand reach, key travel through the finished panel, knob/nut/washer engagement, cable removal, screwdriver access and cover lift-out. A numerical2D packing pass does not satisfy these checks. Print a100 mm scale bar beside the paper layout and verify it with a ruler.

19. **Follow a serviceable assembly order.** Install captive hardware, brackets and feet; rear connector supports; protected power and main tray; display/control boards on the top; encoder hardware/knobs, joystick and caps; labelled harnesses; unpowered travel checks; current-limited powered bench tests; covers. Support an open top on blocks instead of hanging it from its wires. Do not purchase duplicate encoder nuts/washers or workbench tools already owned.

20. **Export only after review.** Close applicable gates in FRIDAY_MANUFACTURING_GATE.md. Preliminary DFM needs an actual assembly STEP in mm, editable Onshape review link and dimensioned PDF front/rear/side/section views, with HOLD notes, quantity1, material, finish and labels. Record model version and matching export revision. No enclosure STEP, native document link, dimensioned manufacturing drawing, physical coupon or native collision pass has been supplied by this guide; those remain the next build tasks.
