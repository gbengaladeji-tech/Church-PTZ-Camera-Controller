# Current requirements

The [final project decisions](engineering/FINAL_PROJECT_DECISIONS.md) are authoritative. No fabrication release exists.

- Sloped desktop metal enclosure; editable Onshape; removable top, rear I/O and bottom service access.
- ServoCity605616 pan/tilt/twist joystick, owned NX4024T032_011 display with no touch dependency.
- 10 CAM +12 selected-camera presets +12 technical keys =34 keys; six encoders.
- Minimum27 red illuminated APEM1KC keys: CAM, presets, FAST/MIDDLE/SLOW, LOCK and AUTO FOCUS.
- Four visual preset groups of three; every preset targets the selected camera.
- AUTO FOCUS below focus knob. No separate zoom keys or large redundant STOP key.
- Four dedicated full-duplex RS-422 ports, separate Ethernet; CAM5-10 disabled until commissioned.
- Neutral/LOCK/CAM-switch stop and re-arm, truthful command/arrival feedback, and supervised fault testing.
- Keep existing Python prototypes, firmware history and design archives. No purchase, supplier message, fabrication or main merge without authorization.

Proposed dimensions and six encoder assignments require owner approval. Camera exposure/WB/ATW/AF feedback and12-slot mapping are not asserted working on the installed Panasonic path. See the [manufacturing gate](engineering/FRIDAY_MANUFACTURING_GATE.md) and [build guide](engineering/ONSHAPE_BUILD_GUIDE.md).

The metal enclosure need not fit the A1Mini print bed. Small coupon/fixture parts may use it. November6 is a target, not confirmed supplier delivery.
